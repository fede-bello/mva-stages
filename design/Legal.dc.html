What this site is

A non-commercial index of research internship offers, kept by MVA students for other students. It is not a service of ENS Paris-Saclay or of the MVA program.

Each entry is a short summary written by us: organization, title, place, dates, stipend when the posting states it, and a link to the original. We do not copy the text of postings. The original posting is always what counts.

Personal data

There are no accounts and no tracking cookies. Offers and reports are stored without any information about who sent them.

We never publish personal email addresses, only role addresses like jobs@ or recrutement@ when a posting gives one. A supervisor's name appears only when the public posting names them.
